02

Turn a visit into a reliable request

Precise ad → matching local/service page → short form OR WhatsApp → received and qualified request → quote → signed job → margin. A WhatsApp click is neither a received conversation nor a client.

EventMeaning
click_whatsapp / click_callLink click; secondary indicator. No client status yet.
lead_receivedServer-confirmed form receipt; unique contact and source kept.
qualified_leadServed area, real need, feasible job, compatible budget and timing.
quote_sent / wonQuote sent then accepted; revenue and variable costs attributed.

GTM + GA4 + Ads and Pixel, without duplicate events. One primary conversion per goal. Pixel and CAPI share the same event_id. Never send names, numbers or free text to GA4 parameters.

05

Google, Meta, TikTok — role of each channel

Google Search

Audit the last 90 days; keep what already produces qualified quotes. Decide by qualified-contact cost, signed jobs and margin — not clicks.

Instagram + Facebook

One Meta set, ads to WhatsApp or a lead form. The two placements are not two budgets. [12]

WhatsApp Business

Conversion destination, qualification and follow-up. A click is not a received message. The pixel does not read WhatsApp chats.

TikTok

Organic reuse first. Paid later only, with a dedicated envelope and enough videos. Ads Manager mins: >20 USD/day ad group; >50 USD campaign. [13] No hidden TikTok in these amounts.

Default: keep Google during the 90-day audit. If no usable conversions after tracking is fixed: a 30-day Meta pilot at 1,000 ILS. At 1,500: one primary channel. At 3,000: Google 1,800 / Meta 1,200, then by margin. 900 ILS ≈ 29.61/day average (900/30.4).
